# Third-party references

This sandbox app is a React/MobileGym adaptation informed by two MIT-licensed projects:

- `muhanking111/ping-an-delivery` (2026), MIT. The cart line schema, checkout totals,
  order lifecycle, refund eligibility, and delivery-state vocabulary are adapted from
  its `mobile-app` and `platform-api` modules.
- `ddeatrr/hungry-cat` / `chanmao-lite`, MIT, copyright 2022 lidongtony. The public Lite
  client's takeout header, category grid, flash-sale shelf, shop list, floating-cart
  treatment, layout proportions, and the JPG assets copied into `assets/` are reused
  and ported from Vue/uni-app to React.

All product names, stores, accounts, addresses, and transaction data are fictional sandbox
data. The app does not include the upstream project's name, logo, contact details, backend,
or any closed-source client code.
